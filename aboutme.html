Introduction

Hello, I'm IronHulk, a Man In A Black Tuxedo, from Oman. Holding several certifications I've been working in cybersecurity for more than eight years, focusing on network security, offensive operations, and building resilient defense architectures for mid to large-scale enterprises. I do both leading teams and doing detailed technical work. I lead security teams, design and manage large-scale security environments, and develop malware for controlled testing purposes. I work where strategy meets action, making sure security plans fit with business goals, risk control, and keeping operations running smoothly. On the defense side, I set up and keep strong security systems. This involves setting up and adjusting EDR tools, configuring firewalls, setting up secure IPsec connections, designing and securing Active Directory, managing PKI and certificates, automating updates and patches, and doing ongoing checks for vulnerabilities. These efforts help meet regulations, keep operations stable, and maintain good security. As a malware developer and creator of custom Command-and-Control (C2) systems, I build tools used for testing attacks, checking defenses, in controlled situations and approved environments. These tools are made to work with today's security measures include strong endpoint and network protection. In red team activity, I focus on getting initial access, privilege escalation, and lateral movement across complex networks. In purple team exercises, I connect offensive discoveries with defensive improvements, making detection better, refining response steps, and boosting overall awareness of threats. I also design and deliver security awareness programs tailored for both technical and non-technical audiences. These programs go beyond theory and include live, controlled demonstrations of selected attack scenarios, showing how real-world threats such as phishing, malware creation, and execution by visualizing attacker behavior in in real time, I help teams understand risk more clearly, recognize early warning signs, and translate awareness into practical defensive actions.
